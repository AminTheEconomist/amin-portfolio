Business Valuation

Ginzu DCF + Monte Carlo

Aswath Damodaran's Ginzu valuation model as one interactive page: a ten-year FCFF DCF with converging growth, margin, tax and cost of capital, failure and NOL adjustments, R&D capitalisation and dilution-adjusted employee options — plus a Monte Carlo layer that samples four correlated value drivers and re-values the company on every trial, a reverse DCF that reads what growth, margin or cost of capital the market price implies, and the multiples the DCF value implies set beside the market's.

My role: the DCF is Damodaran's; the Monte Carlo layer was my idea, added to his model in the original workbook. I directed the web rebuild and did the validation — read the workbook's formulas and VBA, built a cell-level test harness, and reconciled until the two engines agreed (177 cells of the Apple FY2025 case reproduce to machine precision). The judgement in a valuation is choosing and stressing the inputs; the page exists so that part can be done live.
